WHO WE ARE

Precious Care Kit is a menstrual wellness initiative bringing essential menstrual hygiene products together in one convenient care package.

We aim to make menstrual care easier to access while promoting dignity, inclusion and education in homes, schools and communities.

MISSION

To make menstrual hygiene support more accessible through care, dignity, inclusion and education.

VISION

A future where everyone can manage menstruation safely, confidently and without shame.

OUR VALUES

DIGNITY | CARE | INCLUSION | EDUCATION

Purpose

Precious Care Kit provides essential menstrual hygiene products and education to girls and women in need.
We promote confidence, inclusion and dignity through accessible menstrual care and community support.

Activities

  • Menstrual hygiene awareness and education.
  • Community outreach and care-kit support.
  • Collaboration with schools and organizations.
